## Job Statement
When I am looking to scale my business but my current digital presence is underperforming, I want to find a reliable expert who understands my specific problems, so I can confidently invest in a digital solution without fear of wasting money.

## User Persona: "The Busy Founder"
- **Role:** Small-to-Medium Business Owner or Marketing Director.
- **Context:** Browsing on their smartphone (iOS/Android) during a commute or between meetings. High cognitive load, low patience.
- **Goal:** Quickly determine if Wajdan Digital Alchemy is legitimate, what they cost, and how to contact them.

## Current Solution & Pain Points
- **Current:** Browsing traditional agency websites on mobile.
- **Pain:** Tiny text, pinched zooming, huge walls of text, unclear pricing, and complicated multi-page contact forms that are a nightmare to type out on a phone.
- **Consequence:** They bounce within 15 seconds and go to a competitor.
